title: Welcome to AniStream
---


**AniStream** is a lightweight, high-performance desktop application and media manager designed for streaming anime and series from providers such as **AniWorld.to** and **S.TO**. Built with [Tauri](https://tauri.app/) and [Vue 3](https://vuejs.org/), AniStream delivers an ad-free viewing experience, local and remote catalog caching, and flexible deployment options.

## Why AniStream

<Cards>
    <Card
        icon={<LucidePlayCircle />}
        title="Ad-Free Direct Streaming"
    >
        Clean HLS and MP4 playback, no ads, no popups, no redirects. Just the
        player and your video.
    </Card>

    <Card icon={<LucideShield />} title="Built-in Provider Catalogs">
        Native support for **AniWorld.to** (Anime) and **S.TO** (TV Series) and more,
        browsable right inside the app.
    </Card>

    <Card icon={<LucideHistory />} title="Watch History & Progress">
        Series are bookmarked automatically, episode progress is tracked as you
        watch, and playback resumes exactly where you left off across sessions.
    </Card>

    <Card icon={<LucideLanguages />} title="English & German, Fully Localized">
        The entire UI is translated, not bolted on; switch languages without
        losing your place.
    </Card>

    <Card
        icon={<LucideWorkflow />}
        title="Flexible Deployment"
        href="/docs/comparison">
        Run it fully local as a **Standalone** app, or point a thin **Client**
        at your own self-hosted server. Two separate builds, same experience
        
        [See the comparison](/docs/comparison)
    </Card>
</Cards>
