Upgrading your self-hosted **AniStream Server Stack** delivers the latest streaming scrapers, performance enhancements, and new features while preserving all user accounts, watch history, and playlists.

<Callout type="warn">
    **Downgrading:** Downgrading your Data, once an update was applied to your Database is not possible.
</Callout>

---

## Upgrade Workflow

Upgrades follow a structured 4-step sequence:


<Steps>
    <Step>
        Choose running Host Platform (Docker / CapRover)
    </Step>
    <Step>
        Perform update of the host
    </Step>
</Steps>

---

Select your hosting platform to follow its upgrade instructions:
